Plant description

Tap descriptor buttons to add/remove. Hold a button for a plain-language explanation.
1. Overall shape / habit
Habit means the plant's whole-body shape: upright, trailing, climbing, rosette, tree-like, clumping, spreading.
2. Leaves
Leaf clues are often the fastest path: shape, edge, texture, arrangement, color, veins, thickness.
3. Flowers
Flower clues: color, petal count, symmetry, tube/bell/daisy/orchid/pea shape, scent, and cluster pattern.
4. Fruit / seeds / cones
Fruit, seed pods, berries, cones, capsules, and seed heads can identify a plant even without flowers.
5. Stem / trunk / bark
Stem clues: woody vs soft, square vs round, thorns, hairs, milky sap, peeling bark, ridges, color, branching.
6. Environment / placement
Where it grows is a clue: desert room, tropical room, water edge, shaded bed, hanging basket, rock garden, greenhouse.
7. Odd details / photo notes
Odd details can be decisive: smell, spiral growth, tendrils, aerial roots, sticky leaves, dramatic markings, nearby labels.
